What is a Word Counter?

CreatorKit's Word Counter is a free online tool that counts the number of words and characters in your text. It can be useful for YouTube descriptions, social media captions, scripts, articles, assignments and other content.

Why use a Word Counter?

Knowing your word and character count can help you keep your content within a specific limit and make your writing easier to manage.
