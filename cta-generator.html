What is a CTA?

CTA stands for Call To Action. It is a short message that encourages your audience to take an action, such as subscribing, following, commenting, sharing or visiting a page.

Why use a CTA?

A clear call to action can make it easier for viewers to understand what you want them to do after watching or reading your content.
